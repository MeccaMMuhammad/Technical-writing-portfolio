## Corrections and limitations

1. Drug GMP records are governed by applicable *predicate* requirements; Part 11 applicability depends on records/systems and FDA guidance. FDA guidance and ICH models are not universal standalone regulations.
2. Under applicable 21 CFR 211.84, receiving paperwork or QA review alone does not release raw materials from quarantine; QC examination/testing and authorized quality-unit disposition apply.
3. The FDA 2026 QMSR and Computer Software Assurance publications specifically address in-scope medical-device quality/production contexts. They do not convert generic ERP UAT or finished-drug operations into device QMS activities.
4. NIST SP 800-53/53A Release 5.2.0, SP 800-63-4 and SP 800-61 Rev. 3 are current recommended references in their respective domains; federal obligations depend on agency, contract and system scope.
5. Teams federation, meeting screen-control permissions, and separately approved remote-assistance capability are distinct controls. An external call alone cannot establish device control or a backdoor.
6. Color is a document design choice; use explicit status text, semantic headings, table headers, good contrast. Section 508 applies in its federal procurement/ICT context; testing PDF accessibility requires more than a tagged-PDF or text-extraction indicator.
7. All 13 source Word files were individually extended with tailored findings, re-exported into new PDFs, and linked here. No live system tests or external company documentation were used.
