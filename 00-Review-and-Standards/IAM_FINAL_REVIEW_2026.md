# Final IAM supplement and portfolio applicability review — November 2, 2026

## Document naming
The new document is a **Security Control Assessment Report (SAR)** with **IAM gap analysis**, **risk-ranked findings** and an embedded **Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M)**. It is an independent fictional writing sample, not a real SAR for an accredited system.

## Current 2026 primary references
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0; identity and authorization category **PR.AA**.
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, **Release 5.2.0 (August 2025)**, and companion SP 800-53A Release 5.2.0 for assessment approaches. The release updates include software supply-chain topics; it does **not** mean every IAM control was revised in 2025.
- NIST SP 800-63-4 / 63B-4 / 63C-4 **July 2025 final**, with October 2026 implementation hub; describes identity assurance, authentication and federation. No universal private-sector compliance claim.
- **NIST IR 8587 final, September 2026** adds guidance concerning identity tokens, assertions, validation, and life cycle. Relevant to new F-06.
- NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 risk-assessment approach; CISA IAM Best Practices; CISA Zero Trust Maturity Model.
- Federal context only where applicable: FedRAMP 2026 POA&M expectations, 45 CFR 164.308/312 HIPAA security safeguards, 21 CFR Part 11 record-controls scope.

## Editorial and technical review decisions
1. The new report uses simulated findings, explicit synthetic evidence IDs, a defined scope, and open verification statuses; it does not misrepresent work as actually tested.
2. Qualitative rankings, 30/60/90-day milestones, sample populations and success thresholds are clearly company scenario policies, **not mandated by NIST**.
3. Controls and guidance were mapped at a conceptual level, not assessed against licensed text or a real organization's tailored control baseline.
4. The pre-existing 12 portfolio documents remain fictional examples of pharma QA, generic ERP UAT and Windows incident support. QMSR applies to certain medical-device contexts, GCP to applicable clinical trials, and HIPAA to covered entities/business associates, not automatically to these scenarios.
5. Use dark blue headings and black body text, plain severity words, no color-only status meanings. A visual layout check does not establish PDF/UA or Section 508 conformity.
6. Confirm technical execution separately before treating any SOP or scripted diagnostic as a tested operational procedure. No new production tests were performed here.
7. References accessed as public official pages/summaries. No full licensed ISO/IEC compliance assessment or legal opinion was performed. This is a current targeted review, not a universal exhaustive regulatory certification.
