# Federal source reference register — checked November 3, 2026

This portfolio applies **publicly available U.S. federal accessibility guidance** to fabricated demonstration materials. It is not a federal contract deliverable and does not establish compliance.

| Authority | Reference | How used | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. Access Board | [Revised 508 Standards](https://www.access-board.gov/ict/) | Standards and WCAG 2.0 A/AA incorporated requirements for covered federal ICT | Regulation for covered entities / activities |
| Section508.gov | [Electronic documents](https://www.section508.gov/test/documents/) | Document accessibility scope and testing considerations | Government guidance |
| Interagency ICT Baseline | [Electronic documents v1.0](https://ictbaseline.access-board.gov/document-baselines/) | Consistent test categories and evidence expectations | Baseline, not a complete tool-specific method |
| Section508.gov | [Word authoring](https://www.section508.gov/create/documents/) | Heading styles, tables, accessible source files | Guidance |
| Section508.gov | [PDF authoring](https://www.section508.gov/create/pdfs/) | Tagging, reading order, accessibility review | Guidance |
| DHS | [Trusted Tester Program](https://www.section508.gov/test/trusted-tester/) | Context for official web testing methods and certification | Methodology; certification NOT claimed |
| W3C WAI | [Web Accessibility Initiative](https://www.w3.org/WAI/) | Semantic HTML and keyboard-support practices | Technical guidance |
| W3C WCAG | [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) | Supplemental modern web design considerations | Voluntary recommendation unless incorporated elsewhere |

**Interpretive limits:** Section 508 is not a blanket regulation of all private business websites. DHS testing processes are not interchangeable with general automated scanners. Static code inspection is not equivalent to user testing, AT testing, or PDF structural evaluation. The mock scenario is not TSA-specific and does not claim actual agency testing, approvals or analytics.


## November 3, 2026 additional applicability review
The document-specific appendix for each of the five writing samples records applicability and limits. Section 508 is the federal ICT accessibility baseline where applicable; OMB M-23-22 implements federal digital-service direction, while NARA records rules and the Paperwork Reduction Act are conditional on the actual agency workflow. No fictional company has an approved NARA schedule or OMB collection clearance.
