## Review standards and limits

- Pharmaceutical examples: relevant **21 CFR Part 211** responsibilities and record controls, conditional **Part 11** scope, FDA Data Integrity guidance, ICH Q9(R1)/Q10. ICH guidance is not itself a binding law.
- Software/UAT examples: task-based instructions drawing on ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022 and IEC/IEEE 82079-1:2019; neither establishes automatic GxP applicability.
- IT examples: Microsoft command guidance and NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 (2025) incident-response framework; ordinary troubleshooting is distinct from cybersecurity incident response.
- PDFs use black body text and restrained blue section headings; color is not the only carrier of meaning.
- No real company records, patient data, source-code secrets, approvals or signatures are included.
